# Guided intake

The assessment page asks one question group at a time. The left side lists the steps. The center holds the current question. The right side lists saved notes in short bullets and a deterministic adviser line. On a narrow screen the three areas stack.

Save and continue stores the draft without reloading the page and without moving the window back to the top. Back returns to the previous group. Skip is available on Demand and Notes. The step list opens any earlier answer for revision. Save draft and Run Analyze remain on the page.

## Suggestions

Examples stay optional. Choose Examples, then a phrase. The field changes only after Use it. Dismiss leaves the field as it was. Free text is always available. The phrases come from the local example file and follow the industry already typed. They are illustrative examples, not market facts.

## Revenue methods

The revenue step names a method from the industry words:

- Dining, cafe, and similar businesses: covers and average check, using the existing seat formula.
- Services: billable time, or a monthly retainer.
- Retail: transactions times average sale.
- Software: paying customers times monthly price.
- Clinics: visits times fee.
- Anything else: an independent monthly estimate.

The customer can use the recommended method, edit the figures, pick another method, or enter an independent monthly estimate. A monthly worksheet shows the multiplication only after every input is entered. Copying a monthly product into the monthly revenue field happens only when the customer chooses Copy. The seat formula produces a total for the operating days entered. That total is not copied into the monthly field.

No sourced benchmark is stored. The page says so. It does not invent a publication, a date, a geography, or an operating ratio. Downside for the seat formula uses the multiplier the customer entered. An upside figure is not invented.

Changing a supported monthly field still updates the financial preview. The preview is not the official result. Run Analyze stores the official result.

## Adviser

The side panel is labeled deterministic. It does not call a model. Open the Adviser for the existing conversation. A configured provider is used there, when the customer sends a question, through the existing adviser path. This intake does not start a paid call.

## What a label means

- Customer assumption: text or a number the customer entered.
- Customer-marked measured: the monthly checkbox. It is not the verified demand record.
- Illustrative example: a local phrase or a product of entered assumptions.
- Sourced benchmark: not produced on this page.

A blank field stays missing. It is not stored as zero.

## What does not change

The 0.1 formulas, scoring, the official baseline, account isolation, and the unpaid local pilot stay as they were. A customer still cannot set `human_review_approved`. A recommend-against flag is not cleared by a suggestion or a worksheet.

## Limits

The method list is a local map of industry words, not a licensed industry standard. A live model is not called from the intake. Mobile layout stacks the panels; it was not clicked through on a phone. Research, plans, decks, reports, and exports were not redesigned.
