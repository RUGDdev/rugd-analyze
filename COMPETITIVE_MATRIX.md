# Competitive matrix

Reviewed: 2026-11-09. This compares the local RUGD Analyze 0.2 prototype with publicly marketed capabilities of LivePlan, Upmetrics, IdeaBuddy, Esri Business Analyst, BizEquity, and AlphaSense.

A file titled "Consolidated Product Architecture directive" was not in the repository. This matrix follows the governing 0.2 architecture already in force: four service tiers, one assessment application, Market Intelligence with provenance, a future Market Pulse, and Durable Value kept outside this codebase until a versioned interface exists.

Competitor cells are public product positioning, not a licensed feature audit. LivePlan cells follow the public features page, the Standard and Premium help article (updated March 30, 2026), and the May 2026 product note on market research, plan review, and pitch decks. Rough cost is the cost for RUGD to close the gap. It is not a competitor price.
